Business Insights & Recommendations

1. Content Portfolio: Movies represent approximately 69.7% of the dataset. Review audience demand before adjusting investment between Movies and TV Shows.

2. Geographic Strategy: Use country-level content distribution to identify catalogue concentration and potential regional gaps.

3. Genre Strategy: Review leading genres alongside engagement data before deciding future content investments.

4. Content Trends: Yearly catalogue additions can help identify historical patterns. The latest year may be incomplete.

5. Machine Learning: Random Forest achieved 78.78% accuracy and 78.23% weighted F1-score on the test set. Further validation is recommended before deployment.

6. Business Limitation: This dataset describes catalogue metadata, not viewership, revenue, or subscriber satisfaction. Recommendations are exploratory rather than confirmed commercial outcomes.
